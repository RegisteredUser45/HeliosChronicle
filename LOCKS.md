# Frozen locks

Issue resolutions locked for Helios Chronicle. These override ambiguous wording in the original statement where they conflict.

## Issue 1 — Binding definition

**Binding = cosmology catalog test** (recipes / fuel / rares in the fixed game catalog + extractable × accessibility floor).

Spot price never unbinds a stock; it only affects who digs.

## Issue 2 — Depletion / fuse authority

Depletion and fuse are **physics**, not empire- or tech-gated.

Binding uses a **fixed cosmology** catalog.

Optional later star-stabilizer tech may pause or cancel a fuse only; it does not redefine binding.

## Issue 3 — Wilderness

Wilderness suns are **immortal until surveyed OR claimed**; afterward normal depletion / fuse rules apply.

No silent refill.

## Issue 4 — Home / capital fuse pause

**Capital systems ONLY** may pause the fuse while the home flag is held.

Paused capitals **count in the live-system band**.

Re-flag on conquest is OK.

Non-capitals cannot receive home-pause.

## Issue 5 — Clocks

One **master era length**.

Dry-time, fuse length, and research segment time are **editable ratios** of that master length.

## Issue 6 — Spawn tables

Spawn always includes the **full cosmology binding table from day one** (not gated by research / era).

## Issue 7 — Appearance / stages

**No sick / dying appearance stages.**

After depletion there is only a game-served fuse timer, then explode / end.

## Issue 8 — Who drains

**All extraction drains** the binding remainder: state, civilian, foreign, and abandoned automation still running.

## Issue 9 — Atrocity knowledge

Refugees, wrecks, and signals are **real knowledge objects**.

Standing only moves along knowledge paths.

## Issue 10 — Simulation LOD

Simulation LOD is **mandatory early** (coarse ticks for quiet systems / fine for hot).

Cut features that break long headless runs.

## Issue 11 — Chronicle / History (no full replay)

**No tick-perfect recording or replay files.**

Player-facing history is a **Chronicle** of notable moments drawn from the Kernel **EventLog** (soft retention already in A). Do not build a second recording pipeline.

Lean moment row: **tick**, **system** (optional / unknown ok), **kind**, **one-line** summary.

Shell UI: a **history panel of moments** (filterable, click-through to records) — not a replay viewer.
